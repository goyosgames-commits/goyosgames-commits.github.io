Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This policy covers the AI Marquee Media Player app for Android TV ("the app").

What the app accesses with Google sign-in

  • Your Google account email address, to show which account is signed in on the TV.
  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app stores a backup of its settings there: profiles, lists, watch progress and preferences. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

How the data is used and shared

  • The data stays on your TVs and in your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server, and it is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Access tokens are kept only on the TV that signed in.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

When you connect your own media server, IPTV provider or Trakt account, the app talks to those services directly using the details you enter. Their own privacy policies apply.

Removing your data

Sign out in the app's Settings, or remove AI Marquee at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backup itself, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → AI Marquee → Options → Delete hidden app data. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the TV.
