AI Marquee Media Player

A personal media player for Android TV and Google TV.

AI Marquee plays your own media server (Emby), IPTV playlists and antenna channels on the TV, with a shared Continue Watching row, profiles for everyone in the house, and sync between your TVs.

Sign in with Google is optional. It lets AI Marquee keep a backup of your settings in a private app folder in your own Google Drive, so your TVs stay in sync.
